## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info" title="Before You Begin">
Ensure you have:
- A valid email address for sign-up
- Access to your online banking credentials (read-only)
- Supported banks: BBVA, Santander, CaixaBank, or others via PSD2
- Modern browser: Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
</Callout>

## Sign Up for Your 15-Day Free Trial

Start by creating your Orama account. No credit card required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Dashboard" icon="monitor">
    Go to [https://app.orama.ai](https://app.orama.ai) and click **Prueba Gratis**.

    You'll land on the registration form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Details" icon="user">
    Provide your email, company name, and password. Accept the terms.

    Verify your email via the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Onboarding" icon="check-circle">
    Answer quick questions about your business size and treasury needs.

    Access your dashboard in seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="success">
Account created! You're now ready to connect banks and manage treasury.
</Callout>

## Navigate the Initial Dashboard

Upon login, you'll see:

- **Overview panel**: Current cash position summary
- **Bank connections**: Section to add accounts
- **Reports**: Quick access to cash flow and forecasts

Use the sidebar to jump between **Tesorería**, **Informes**, and **Configuración**.

## Securely Sync Bank Accounts

Orama uses secure PSD2 connections—no credentials stored.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="BBVA" icon="banknote">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select BBVA" icon="arrow-right">
        In dashboard, click **Conectar Banco** > BBVA.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authorize Access">
        Redirected to BBVA login. Grant read-only access.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm Sync">
        Transactions sync automatically. View last 90 days.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Santander" icon="banknote">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Santander">
        Choose Santander from bank list.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Multi-Factor Auth">
        Complete bank's MFA challenge.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Data Import">
        Balances and movements appear instantly.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CaixaBank" icon="banknote">
    Follow similar flow: Select > Login > Authorize > Sync.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
Sync refreshes daily or manually. Supports 50+ Spanish banks.
</Callout>

## Create Your First Cash Flow Report

With banks connected:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Period" icon="calendar">
    Go to **Informes** > **Flujo de Caja**. Choose dates (e.g., last 30 days).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Preview" icon="bar-chart">
    Click **Generar**. See inflows, outflows, and net position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export or Share" icon="download">
    Download as PDF/Excel or share via link.
  </Step>
</Steps>
