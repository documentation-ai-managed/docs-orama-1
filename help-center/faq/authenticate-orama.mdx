## Overview

To use Orama, you need to log in to your dashboard.

## Steps

1. Go to `https://app.orama.dev`  
2. Click "Sign in"
3. Use your email or GitHub account
4. Approve login if asked

<Callout kind="info">
If you don't have an account, click "Sign up" first.
</Callout>
