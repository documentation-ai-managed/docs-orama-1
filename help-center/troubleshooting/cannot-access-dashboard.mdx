---
title: Cannot Access Orama Dashboard
description: Quick steps to resolve Orama dashboard access issues.
---

## Symptoms

- Getting a blank page
- Error: "Access denied" or "Network error"

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Login Status">
    Log out and log back in at `https://app.orama.dev`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear Cache">
    Clear browser cache and cookies, then reload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try Another Browser">
    Test access in Chrome, Firefox, or Safari.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Still blocked? Contact support@orama.dev.
</Callout>
