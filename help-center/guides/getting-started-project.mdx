---

## Start a Project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Orama">
    Go to `https://app.orama.dev` and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New Project">
    Click "New Project"
    <br/>Enter a project name, e.g. "Docs Demo"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Documentation">
    Click "Add Document"
    <br/>Start writing or import markdown files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Hit "Publish" to make your docs live.
  </Step>
</Steps>
